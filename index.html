Add-ons
MaiPai Catalognew features and abilities for Home and Bot: plugins, apps, companions and integrations, reviewed and signed
  • The Stack runs the AI: it installs the engines and models, sizes them to your computer, and keeps them healthy. You can run it by itself.
  • Home sits on top and adds the people: profiles, memory, companions, apps, and the rules for kids.
  • Bot carries its own Stack and its own copy of Home's brain, so it works alone and joins the family when it is in range.
  • Go and Desktop are how you reach Home and Bot from a phone, a TV or a computer. They never talk to the Stack directly.
  • The Catalog is where Home and Bot get new features and abilities, each one reviewed and signed before you can add it.
