Funnels

The robot is event-driven already: services publish on a bus, other services subscribe. That part was never the problem. The problem was that several subscribers each turned the same raw events into their own idea of what was true, and those ideas drifted.

A funnel is the missing middle: one component that reduces the raw events into a single resolved state and publishes that. Everything downstream renders it.

What this is called

It is event-driven design, which the robot already had: services publish events on a bus and whatever cares subscribes. Nothing here replaces that.

What was missing is the piece with several names in the wild, all describing the same move: a reducer (the events are folded into one state), a projection (the stream is projected into a current view), a single source of truth for that fact, or an aggregator. “Funnel” is the word we use here because it describes the shape: many events in the top, one answer out of the bottom, and everything downstream drinks from the same place.

The distinction worth holding onto: publishing events is not the pattern. Every one of the bugs below happened on a healthy event bus. The pattern is that the reduction happens once, rather than once per subscriber.

Why, in the robot’s own scars

Every one of these was found by a person looking at the robot, not by a test, because each surface was individually correct:

  • “Listening” meant two things at once. The screen read it as brain == "engaged"; the microphone ring read it as “the follow-up window is open”. So the ring lit beside a green idle tick on the panel, and the robot was telling the family two different things about whether it was hearing them.
  • A lightning bolt meant charging in two places, one of which had a battery column already saying it.
  • A care-stop was a worded banner on the screen and nothing at all on any other surface.

Five surfaces (screen, mic ring, HEART knob, underglow, face) each derived the conversation from voice/speaking, voice/window, voice/wake and the rest. Five derivations of one truth drift exactly as five copies of anything drift.

The rule

If two consumers would have to agree about something, resolve it once and publish the answer. If it is genuinely about one consumer, leave it there.

The split is easy to apply in practice:

Belongs in the funnelBelongs in the consumer
Is the robot listening, thinking, speaking?Which glyph, how big, what colour
Is the microphone live?How fast the ring breathes
Did a care-stop just happen?How long the stop sign holds on THIS surface
Is it recording?Whether this surface can show privacy at all

What a funnel looks like

robot/presence/ is the worked example.

  • It is the only subscriber to its raw topics. If a consumer still reads voice/speaking, the funnel has failed and the drift is already back.
  • It publishes on change, not on a timer, so consumers can treat every message as an edge and the bus carries no heartbeat nobody reads. A slow tick exists only because some state expires on its own (a stop that has been held long enough).
  • Its output is a plain message on one topic, so a new surface costs one subscription and nothing else.
  • Separate questions stay separate fields. conversation says what the robot is doing; mic_live says whether the microphone is open and will act. A privacy indicator has to be right even while the activity indicator says “speaking”, so folding those two into one enum would be a bug waiting.

Testing one

The coverage moves with the logic. Three kinds of test:

  1. The resolution, in the funnel’s own test file: raw events in, one state out, including the decay of anything held.
  2. The rendering, in each consumer’s tests, driven by the resolved state rather than by raw events. If a consumer’s test still has to publish a raw topic to light something up, the derivation crept back in.
  3. A guard that asserts no consumer imports or subscribes to the raw topics. That is the test that keeps the pattern true a year from now, and it is three lines of inspect.getsource.

When NOT to funnel

A funnel that grows to hold everything is just a god object with a nicer name. Battery percentage is not in presence: it has exactly one source already (power/state), nobody derives it, and it never drifted. The pattern earns its keep when derivation is duplicated, not merely when data is shared.
